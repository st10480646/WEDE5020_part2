Who We Are

OVRSIZ was born in 2026 out of a frustration with restrictive, fast-fashion silhouettes. We wanted clothes that feel as good to skate in as they do to chill in.

Mission & Vision

  • Mission: To provide premium, gender-neutral, oversized garments that empower self-expression.
  • Vision: To become the leading streetwear staple for the global youth culture.
